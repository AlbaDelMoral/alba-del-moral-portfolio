Stories of a Walk is a photobook project that captures the experience of strolling through New York City. For this project, we worked with photography collections from the New York Public Library, narrowing them down to select images that aligned with our vision. I chose photographs from A Trip Along the Yangtze River, 1999, by Dylan Stone, using them to convey the rhythm and flow of a walk through the city.

The photobook is designed as a series of smaller booklets housed within a larger book, with each booklet representing a specific moment in time during the walk. Colored paper separates each section, and the booklets are hand-sewn with thread. On the cover, I included a short "poem" that is repeated on each booklet's cover, emphasizing the passage of time and the pace of the walk.

For the hardcover, I used chipboard wrapped in textured cardboard, laser-printed with the title and the silhouette of an old man at the bottom. The entire book is held together with a thick black rubber band, giving it a cohesive and tactile finish.
